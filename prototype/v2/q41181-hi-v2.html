The golden eagle (Aquila chrysaetos) is a bird of prey living in the Northern Hemisphereउत्तरी गोलार्ध. It is the most widely distributed species of eagleमहाश्येन. Like all eagles, it belongs to the family Accipitridaeऐकीपिट्रीडाए. They are one of the best-known birds of prey in the Northern Hemisphere. These birds are dark brown, with lighter golden-brown plumage on their napesडब. Immature eagles of this species typically have white on the tail and often have white markings on the wings. Golden eagles use their agility and speed combined with powerful feet and large, sharp talons to hunt a variety of prey, mainly haresखरहा, rabbitsखरगोश, marmotsफीया, and other ground squirrels.

¶ +3

Golden eagles maintain home ranges or territories that may be as large as 200 km2 (77 sq mi). They build large nestsgosala in cliffs and other high places to which they may return for several breeding years. Most breeding activities take place in the spring; they are monogamous and may remain together for several years or possibly for life. Females lay up to four eggs, and then incubate them for six weeks. Typically, one or two young survive to fledge in about three months. These juvenile golden eagles usually attain full independence in the fall, after which they wander widely until establishing a territory for themselves in four to five years.

Once widespread across the Holarcticहोलार्कटिक, it has disappeared from many areas that are heavily populated by humans. Despite being extirpated from or uncommon in some of its former range, the species is still widespread, being present in sizeable stretches of Eurasiaयूरेशिया, North Americaउत्तर अमेरिका, and parts of North Africaउत्तर अफ़्रीका. It is the largest and least populous of the five species of true accipitrid to occur as a breeding species in both the Palearcticपेलार्कटिक and the Nearcticनियार्कटिक जैवभूक्षेत्र.

For centuries, this species has been one of the most highly regarded birds used in falconryश्येनपालन. Because of its hunting prowess, the golden eagle is regarded with great mystic reverence in some ancient, tribal cultures. It is one of the most extensively studied species of raptor in the world in some parts of its range, such as the Western United Statesपश्चिमी संयुक्त राज्य and the Western Palearctic.
